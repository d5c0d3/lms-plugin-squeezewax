PLUGIN_SQUEEZEWAX_NAME
	EN	SqueezeWax

PLUGIN_SQUEEZEWAX_DESC
	EN	Links your physical record collection to your LMS library.

PLUGIN_SQUEEZEWAX_TAGNAMES
	EN	Discogs release ID tags

PLUGIN_SQUEEZEWAX_TAGNAMES_DESC
	EN	Tag names in your files that hold a Discogs release ID. There is no standard name, and it differs between formats, so more than one may be needed. Leave blank to disable tag matching.

PLUGIN_SQUEEZEWAX_TAGNAMES_ORDER
	EN	Order matters only for reporting which tag a match came from. If two tags name different releases, the album goes to the review queue rather than one winning.

PLUGIN_SQUEEZEWAX_DETECT
	EN	Detect tag names

PLUGIN_SQUEEZEWAX_DETECT_DESC
	EN	Reads a sample of albums and reports which tags hold something that looks like a Discogs release ID.

PLUGIN_SQUEEZEWAX_DETECT_BUTTON
	EN	Detect

PLUGIN_SQUEEZEWAX_DETECT_RUNNING

PLUGIN_SQUEEZEWAX_DETECT_FORMATS
	EN	Formats sampled

PLUGIN_SQUEEZEWAX_DETECT_TAG
	EN	Tag name

PLUGIN_SQUEEZEWAX_DETECT_COUNT
	EN	Albums

PLUGIN_SQUEEZEWAX_DETECT_EXAMPLE
	EN	Example ID

PLUGIN_SQUEEZEWAX_DETECT_COPY
	EN	Copy the tag names you want into the boxes above, then save.

PLUGIN_SQUEEZEWAX_DETECT_NONE
	EN	No tags holding a Discogs release ID were found in the sample.

PLUGIN_SQUEEZEWAX_DETECT_OTHER
	EN	Other tags holding a number, shown in case one of them is your release ID. These were not confirmed, because a bare number on its own is not evidence.
