PLUGIN_SQUEEZEWAX_TOKEN_DESC
	EN	Required for collection sync and the owned badge. Generate one at your Discogs Developer Settings and paste it here. This token is an unscoped credential for your whole Discogs account: whoever has it can do anything you can do on Discogs, including creating Marketplace listings and editing orders, not just read your collection. It is stored in this server's preferences file in plain text, not encrypted. If you revoke it, generate a new one and paste it in again.

PLUGIN_SQUEEZEWAX_SYNC
	EN	Discogs collection

PLUGIN_SQUEEZEWAX_SYNC_DESC
	EN	SqueezeWax reads your Discogs collection to work out which albums in this library you own on record. It only counts what is there; nothing about your collection is stored, and your library is not changed. This happens automatically after a library rescan and on the interval below, and you can run it now with the button.

PLUGIN_SQUEEZEWAX_SYNC_BUTTON
	EN	Sync collection now

PLUGIN_SQUEEZEWAX_SYNC_BUTTON_RUNNING
	EN	Syncing...

PLUGIN_SQUEEZEWAX_SYNC_INTERVAL
	EN	Sync interval (seconds)

PLUGIN_SQUEEZEWAX_SYNC_INTERVAL_DESC
	EN	How long to wait between automatic collection syncs. The default is 86400 (once a day), and the minimum is 3600 (once an hour). A sync costs one Discogs request per 100 items in your collection, so there is little to gain from a short interval.
