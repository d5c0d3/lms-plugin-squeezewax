Plugin for Lyrion Music Server 1.0.0b3 · beta

Your shelf, inside your library.

SqueezeWax reads your Discogs record collection and works out which albums in your LMS library are records you own. Owned albums say so in their menu, link to the release on Discogs, and appear in a “Records I own” view.

Add this under Settings → Plugins → Additional Repositories

https://d5c0d3.github.io/lms-plugin-squeezewax/repo.xml

Read the six-step setup →

How ownership is decided

From your Discogs collection — the records on your shelf.

You own a pressing

The release ID in your file tags is in your collection — the exact pressing you own.

You own a version

The tagged release isn’t in your collection, but its master release is — another pressing of the same record.

A version, no tag needed

Exactly one collection entry has the same album title and artist. Anything less certain goes to the review queue.

Requirements

What you need

  • Lyrion Music Server 8.4 or newer — tested on 9.1.1.
  • A Discogs account, with your records in your collection.
  • A Discogs personal access token.
  • For exact pressings: a tag holding the Discogs release ID on every track — 123456, [r123456] or a release URL.

No other plugins needed. HTTPS comes from LMS on macOS and from the system Perl elsewhere.

First-time setup

Six steps to “Records I own”

  1. 01Add the repository URL above and install SqueezeWax from the plugin list.
  2. 02Paste your Discogs token and press Test token.
  3. 03Enter your release-ID tag name, or press Detect and copy what it finds. Save.
  4. 04Run a library rescan: Basic Settings → Rescan Media Library → “Look for new and changed media files”.
  5. 05Wait until the settings page no longer shows “Master releases”. If it says “paused”, press Sync collection now.
  6. 06Press Sync collection now once. “You own a version” results appear after this sync.
This is a beta. Updates may change the SqueezeWax database, and you cannot go back to an older version.

Using it

Where it shows up in LMS

Album & track menus

Links to your pressing and to all versions on Discogs. Players show “You own a pressing” or “You own a version”.

Records I own

A library view and My Music entry showing only the albums you own, in the normal album grid.

Pressing details

Label, catalogue number, format, haves and wants, rating and copies for sale — fetched only when you open the submenu.

Review queue

Albums it couldn’t decide. Show tags, choose from your collection, or remove a match.

Your collection is read during each library scan. There is no timer: a record you add on Discogs shows up after the next scan or “Sync collection now”. Read the user guide →

Data & privacy

Conclusions are stored. Discogs data isn’t.

Fetched, then discarded

Your username, currency and whole collection at each sync, 100 items a page. The collection is held in memory for one pass. Details submenus are cached in memory for up to 2 minutes.

Kept in squeezewax.db

Release and master IDs, whether you own each album and how it was matched — plus details from your own LMS library. No Discogs titles, artists or tracklists.

Your token

Stored in plain text in LMS preferences. Uninstalling does not delete it — revoke it in Discogs Developer Settings.

Requests stay within Discogs’ limit of 60 a minute. A collection of N items costs about N/100 requests per sync.
