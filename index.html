Plugin for Lyrion Music Server  

Your shelf, inside your library.

SqueezeWax reads your Discogs record collection and works out which albums in your LMS library are records you own. Owned albums say so in their menu, link to the release on Discogs, and appear in a “Records I own” view.

Add this under Settings → Plugins → Additional Repositories

How ownership is decided

On Discogs, every pressing of an album (the original LP, a CD, a reissue) is its own release. All of them together form the album’s master release. SqueezeWax compares your files with your Discogs collection:

You own a pressing

Your files are tagged with a Discogs release, and that exact release is in your collection.

You own a version

Your collection has a different pressing of the same album, such as the CD when your files are tagged with the LP.

A version, no tag needed

Your files have no Discogs tag, but exactly one record in your collection has the same title and artist. Anything less clear-cut goes to the review queue, which says what to change.

Requirements

  • Lyrion Music Server — tested on 9.1.1.
  • A Discogs account, with your records in your collection.
  • A Discogs personal access token.
  • For the exact pressing: a tag on every track with the Discogs release number, written as 123456, as [r123456], or as the release’s web address.
It assumes a well-tagged library and a well-kept Discogs collection. SqueezeWax only compares the two. A record missing from your collection, or an album without the right tags, is not recognised as yours. What to tag →

No other plugins needed. On Linux and Windows, secure connections rely on the system’s Perl; see the README.

First-time setup

  1. 01Add the repository URL above and install SqueezeWax from the plugin list.
  2. 02Paste your Discogs token and press Test token.
  3. 03Enter the name of the tag that holds the release number, for example DISCOGS_RELEASE_ID, or press Detect and copy what it finds. Save.
  4. 04Run a library rescan: Basic Settings → Rescan Media Library → “Look for new and changed media files”.
  5. 05SqueezeWax now looks up each album’s master release in the background. Wait until the settings page no longer shows “Master releases”. If it says “paused”, press Sync collection now.
  6. 06Only if your files have no master-release tag: press Sync collection now once after step 05. Albums whose files carry a master tag already show “You own a version” after the rescan.

Tagging for SqueezeWax

These tags are what SqueezeWax reads. Set the tag name for the release number under Settings.

  • One album, one release numberEvery file of an album carries the same Discogs release number, in the tag name set in SqueezeWax’s settings.
  • Multi-disc setsPut the same release number on every disc.
  • Your own compilations and mixtapesLeave the release number out of the tag name SqueezeWax reads. To keep each track’s number, put it in another tag name.
  • Master release (optional)A master-release tag (DISCOGS_MASTER_RELEASE_ID, as foobar2000’s Discogs component writes it, or DISCOGS_MASTER_ID / DISCOGS MASTER ID) lets “You own a version” show right after the rescan, without waiting for the background master lookup.
  • Artist namesIf the artist is spelled differently in your files and in your Discogs collection, change one so they match.
  • Streaming albumsThey can’t be tagged. SqueezeWax matches them by title and artist only.

After tagging, rescan with “Look for new and changed media files”.

Where it shows up in LMS

Album & track menus

Links to your pressing and to all versions on Discogs. Players show “You own a pressing” or “You own a version”.

Records I own

A library view and My Music entry showing only the albums you own, in the normal album grid.

Pressing details

Label, catalogue number, format, how many Discogs users have it and want it, rating and copies for sale — fetched only when you open the submenu.

Review queue

Albums it couldn’t decide, each with what to change in your tags or your Discogs collection.

Good to know

Discogs changes wait for a scan

A record you add or remove on Discogs shows up after the next library scan or “Sync collection now”. There is no timer.

A wrong match stays until you fix it

An album wrongly marked as owned by the title-and-artist match cannot be unmarked in the plugin. Fix the collection or the tags.

Removing a tag doesn’t undo a match

An album that was identified from its Discogs tag keeps that identification when the tag is removed. Retag it with the right release number instead.

Some albums need your help

Compilations, and albums that match several records in your collection, go to the review queue. It says what to change in your tags or your collection; there is no way to link an album by hand.

Your token can do anything you can

It is stored unencrypted on the server, and uninstalling SqueezeWax does not remove it. Revoke it in Discogs Developer Settings when you stop.

No way back

Updates may change the SqueezeWax database, and an older version cannot read it. Tested on one Linux and one Docker server; Windows, macOS and piCorePlayer are untested.

What SqueezeWax fetches and stores is listed under Data and privacy in the README.

Questions or problems?

Ask questions and share your setup in the forum thread on the Lyrion forums.

For a bug, open an issue with your LMS version, OS and SqueezeWax version, plus the log lines mentioning Plugins::SqueezeWax (set plugin.squeezewax to INFO in LMS’s logging settings first). Never include your Discogs token.
